## How to join
1. Read the guide in the **community** repo.
2. Pick an `onramp` task on the **Onramp** board.
3. Comment "I'll take it" and a lead will assign it to you.

## Levels
| Level | How you get there | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| Participant | Finish 1 onramp task | Take onramp tasks |
| Contributor | ~5 tasks | Medium tasks, triage issues |
| Team member | Invited by a team lead | Work on a mini team board |
| Team lead | Invited by admins | Run a mini team |
